Privacy &
cookies.

Last updated: July 2026

This is a small, one-person studio site. Here's a plain-language account of what happens with your data when you visit, in full instead of legal boilerplate.

What this site collects

If you fill in the contact form, I collect your name, email or WhatsApp number, business type and whatever you write in the message. That's used for one purpose: to reply to you about your project. Nothing else is collected automatically. This site has no analytics and no advertising trackers.

Where your message goes

The contact form is sent through two processors so I can receive it by email:

  • EmailJS, delivers your message to my inbox.
  • Formspree, used only for the "find your fit" quiz, to notify me when someone completes it.

Both providers process the message on my behalf and do not use it for their own marketing.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not set tracking cookies. The only thing stored in your browser is a small entry in local storage that remembers whether you accepted or dismissed the cookie notice, so it doesn't reappear on every visit. It never leaves your device.

Fonts and third-party requests

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, and the animated hero background and buttons run entirely in your browser. Portfolio photos are served from this site or from Unsplash.

How long messages are kept

Contact form messages live in my email inbox for as long as needed to complete your project, then are deleted. You can ask for your message to be deleted at any time.

Your rights

You can ask what data I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Just email me.
